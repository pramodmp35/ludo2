Ludo Royale

Local multiplayer · Red + Yellow favourites — but Green + Blue fight hard!

How to Play

Classic Ludo with a few extra twists

🎯 Goal

Bring all 4 of your tokens from your yard, around the board, and into the centre home. First team to finish wins.

👑 Favourites Mode

  • Red and Yellow are always teammates — and always favourites to win.
  • They roll smart dice — 6 when needed, capture rolls when available, home rolls when possible.
  • Their tokens can be captured — Green + Blue will absolutely take their shots.
  • Green + Blue play an adaptive AI: they catch up when behind, get aggressive when far behind, and dial back only when they threaten to overtake.

🤝 Pair Rules

  • Each pair shares the turn — both dice glow when it's your team's turn.
  • Once a player gets all 4 tokens home, they stop rolling. Their dice greys out with a ✓ DONE badge, and only their partner continues rolling for the team.
  • Example: Red finishes all 4 → Yellow carries the team solo. When Yellow also finishes, the team wins.

🎲 Rolling

  • Tap your dice — the glowing one in your corner — to roll.
  • Roll a 6 to release a token from your yard.
  • Rolling a 6 grants an extra turn.

⚔️ Capturing

  • Land exactly on an opponent's token to send it back to its yard.
  • Capturing earns you an extra turn.
  • Reaching home also grants another roll.

⌨️ Controls

Space rolls the active dice · click a glowing token to move it.
